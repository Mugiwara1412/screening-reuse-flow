Screening Reuse — Revised Flow

Based on Marcus's feedback: auto-reuse screening submissions for duplicate questionnaire invites instead of sending multiple full invitation emails. Reduces candidate email fatigue and recruiter overhead.

Current vs Proposed Approach

Current / Emma's Design

Candidate-Initiated Reuse

  • Every invite sends a full invitation email to the candidate
  • Candidate clicks link, lands on screening page
  • If prior results exist, a modal pops up: "Submit existing" or "Record again"
  • Candidate must take action for every single invite
  • Problem: N jobs = N emails + N candidate decisions
  • Problem: Recruiter sees "Invited" status for all pending screenings — cognitive overload
  • Problem: Candidates may ignore or unsubscribe from emails
Proposed / Marcus's Approach

System-Initiated Auto-Reuse

  • 1st invite: Normal invitation email → candidate completes screening
  • 2nd+ invite (same questionnaire): Send a compact "reuse notification" email
  • Notification says: "Your submission will be automatically reused for this job"
  • System auto-submits the prior results — no candidate action needed
  • Benefit: Fewer emails, lighter emails, less friction
  • Benefit: Recruiter sees auto-completed status immediately
  • Opt-out: Candidate can re-trigger screening from "Review Screening" email

Detailed Scenario Walkthroughs

Happy Path

Auto-Reuse After Completion

Candidate completed screening for Job A. Gets invited to Job B with the same questionnaire. System auto-reuses — candidate is simply notified.

01
Recruiter Invites candidate to Job A screening (Questionnaire Q1)
02
System Sends standard Invitation Email for Job A
03
Candidate Completes voice screening for Job A
04
Recruiter Invites same candidate to Job B screening (same Q1)
05
System Detects duplicate: same candidate + same questionnaire + prior submission exists
06
System Sends "Reuse Notification" email (not a full invitation) Email lists Job B and explains the prior submission from Job A will be reused
07
System Auto-copies Job A submission to Job B — status becomes "Completed (Reused)"
08
Recruiter Sees Job B screening as completed; can evaluate immediately
Opt-Out Path

Candidate Wants to Redo Screening

Candidate receives reuse notification but decides to provide fresh answers for Job B (e.g., different context or wants to improve).

01
System Sends Reuse Notification email for Job B
02
Candidate Opens the "Review Screening" email and clicks "Redo Screening" This link is available in the review screening email template
03
System Invalidates the auto-reused submission for Job B
04
System Sends fresh Invitation Email for Job B screening
05
Candidate Completes new voice screening for Job B
06
Recruiter Sees updated screening for Job B with new submission Q: Does the recruiter get notified that the candidate chose to redo?
Timing Edge Case

2nd Invite Before 1st Screening Completed

Candidate is invited to Job B (same Q1) while the Job A screening is still pending (not yet completed).

01
Recruiter Invites candidate to Job A screening (Q1) — candidate hasn't started yet
02
Recruiter Invites same candidate to Job B screening (same Q1)
03
System Detects: same Q1 but NO prior submission yet
04
System Sends "Pending Reuse" email for Job B "You have been invited for screening for Job B. This uses the same questionnaire as Job A. Once you complete Job A's screening, your answers will be automatically applied to Job B."
05
Candidate Completes screening for Job A
06
System Auto-propagates Job A submission to Job B Both Job A and Job B screening are now "Completed"
Batch Scenario

Multiple Jobs Invited Simultaneously

Candidate is invited to 5 jobs at once, all using the same questionnaire Q1. (Common in retail / high-volume hiring.)

01
System / AE Automation Engine invites candidate to Jobs A, B, C, D, E — all with Q1
02
System Sends 1 full Invitation Email (for Job A — the first/primary)
03
System Sends 1 consolidated Reuse Notification for Jobs B, C, D, E "You've also been invited for 4 other jobs that share the same screening. Your answers will be reused for all of them."
04
Candidate Completes screening once (for Job A)
05
System Auto-propagates submission to Jobs B, C, D, E All 5 screenings are now "Completed (Reused)" — recruiters can evaluate
06
Candidate (optional) Can redo screening for any specific job from the review email

Open Questions — For Samanth & Ramna Discussion

Data / Timestamps

What is the "submission date" for reused screenings?

When Job B auto-reuses Job A's submission, should the submission date be: (a) the original Job A completion date, (b) the date the reuse was triggered, or (c) both shown? This affects SLA tracking, compliance reporting, and recruiter dashboards.

P0 — Must resolve
Candidate UX

How do we show screening status while Job A is still pending?

If Job B is invited but Job A screening hasn't been completed yet, what status does Job B show? "Pending Reuse"? "Awaiting Primary Screening"? The candidate and recruiter both need clarity on what's blocking.

P0 — Must resolve
Email / Notifications

Should reuse notifications be individual or batched?

If a candidate is invited to 10 jobs with the same Q1, do they get 9 individual reuse notification emails or 1 consolidated email listing all jobs? Batching is cleaner but requires aggregation logic and timing windows.

P0 — Must resolve
Recruiter Experience

How does the recruiter know a screening was reused vs. freshly completed?

Should the CRM/screening dashboard display a "Reused" badge? Should evaluators be told this is a reused submission? This affects evaluation context — the answers may have been given for a different job.

P1 — Important
Edge Case

What happens when the candidate redoes screening — does it update all reused jobs?

If Job B and Job C both reused Job A's submission, and the candidate redoes the screening for Job B, does Job C also get the new answers? Or does each job operate independently after reuse?

P0 — Must resolve
Edge Case

What if Job A's screening expires before the candidate completes it?

Job B was told "your answers from Job A will be reused." But Job A expired. Does Job B now get a full new invitation? Does the candidate need to be re-invited? How is the recruiter notified?

P1 — Important
Compliance / Legal

Does auto-reuse require explicit candidate consent?

In some jurisdictions (GDPR, EEOC), candidates may need to explicitly opt in to sharing their screening data across jobs. Is a notification email sufficient, or do we need an affirmative consent step?

P0 — Must resolve
Data / Architecture

Is the reused screening a copy or a reference?

When we "reuse" a submission for Job B, do we create a copy of the recording/responses (immutable), or just link to Job A's data? Copies are safer (Job A deletion doesn't affect Job B) but consume more storage.

P1 — Important
Candidate UX

Where does the "Redo Screening" option live?

Marcus mentioned it's in the "Review Screening" email template. But should it also be available on the screening landing page? What's the time window for redoing — indefinitely, or before the expiry date of that job's screening invite?

P1 — Important
Recruiter Experience

Can the recruiter override and force a fresh screening?

If a recruiter specifically wants fresh answers for Job B (e.g., different hiring manager preferences), can they disable the auto-reuse for a specific invite? This might need a per-invite toggle.

P2 — Nice to have
Edge Case

What about partially completed screenings?

If the candidate started but didn't finish Job A's screening (e.g., dropped mid-call for voice agent), and then gets invited to Job B — does the system wait for completion, or treat it as "no prior submission"?

P1 — Important
Email / Notifications

Does the recruiter receive a "Screening Completed" email for reused screenings?

When Job B's screening is auto-filled via reuse, does the recruiter get the standard "Screening Completed" notification? Or a different "Screening Reused" notification? Notification fatigue is a concern for the recruiter too.

P1 — Important
Data / Evaluation

How do evaluations and AI scores interact with reused screenings?

If Job A's screening was already evaluated/scored, do those evaluations carry over to Job B? Per existing behavior, evaluations do NOT transfer. But for voice agent screening with AI-generated scores, this could be different.

P1 — Important
Compliance / Audit

How do we handle the 6-month reuse window?

Current KB article mentions reuse is available for submissions within the past 6 months. Does the same window apply here? What if Job B is invited 7 months after Job A was completed — no reuse, or configurable window?
